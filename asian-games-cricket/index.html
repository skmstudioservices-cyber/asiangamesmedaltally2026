🥇 INDIA WOMEN — GOLD MEDAL

India's women's cricket team won the gold medal at the Asian Games 2026, defeating Sri Lanka by 147 runs in the final on 22 September 2026. It is India's second consecutive women's cricket gold at the Asian Games.

🏏 India Men — Quarter-final, 28 September

The men's competition began on 24 September with the group stage. India, one of the seeded teams, enter directly at the quarter-final stage on 28 September. Their opponent is decided by the group-stage results.

  • Men's format: T20 · quarter-finals from 28 September
  • Recent form: India's men won the T20 cricket gold at the 2023 Hangzhou Asian Games
  • Note: The senior ODI squad is playing West Indies at home in the same window, so the Asian Games T20 side is led by Shreyas Iyer

Why cricket at the Asian Games matters

Cricket returned to the Asian Games in 2010. Both Indian teams won gold at Hangzhou 2023, and searches for Asian Games cricket have surged again this week as the men's tournament approaches the knockout stage.
